05 — Live meetings

It sits in on your calls.

Record a meeting and your assistant transcribes it as it happens. While you're talking, it watches the transcript in the background and speaks up in your chat only when something is worth catching — a date that moved, a promise you just made. Afterwards you've got the full transcript and it can act on what it heard.

Todos

Nothing falls through the cracks.

The hard part of Getting Things Done was never the method — it was keeping the list honest. Chatty ships a full GTD system, and your assistant does the upkeep: catching things wherever they land, sorting them into the right bucket, and telling you what's gone stale.

01

Capture from anywhere

Bookmark the no-login capture page on your phone — type or dictate, hit send, done. Text your bot "capture buy milk" on Telegram and it's logged instantly, no AI in the loop. Or let your assistant pull todos out of your email, your calls, and your chats on its own.

02

Triaged for you

An inbox full of half-sentences isn't a system. Your assistant works the inbox with you — turning vague notes into real next actions, tagging them by context, grouping them under projects, and tracking what you're waiting on from other people.

03

Reviewed weekly

The review is the part everyone skips. There's a page for it, and your assistant comes prepared — what closed, what's stalled, what's been sitting untouched for two weeks, and what you said you'd do and haven't.

Agents

One is plenty. But you can have more.

Most people need exactly one assistant, and that's how Chatty starts — give it a name and it walks you through training in a natural conversation. Or import an existing agent from OpenClaw and skip training entirely. When one isn't enough, add another with its own personality, knowledge, and tools — they share what they know.

You own everything your agents learn. Every conversation, every memory, every piece of knowledge lives on your machine or your server — not in someone else's cloud. Switch providers, move hosts, or go offline. Your agents and everything they know come with you.
